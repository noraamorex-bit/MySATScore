What the practice test does

The goal is that nothing about the experience surprises you on test day except the questions themselves.

  • The clock belongs to the server — refreshing, closing the tab, or sleeping the device cannot buy extra time.
  • Module 2 is routed from your module 1 performance by a deterministic, difficulty-weighted policy. Difficulty is never shown during a test.
  • Mark for review, an answer eliminator, a question navigator, and a check-your-work screen before every submission.
  • An on-screen calculator and the formula reference sheet, on Math only.
  • Section scores on the 200–800 scale and a 400–1600 total, with the routed form affecting the conversion.
  • Results by domain, by difficulty, and by skill, with every question reviewable and explained.
  • Missed questions can be retried untimed, without touching your score.

About the questions

Every question is original, written for this project. No College Board material is reproduced. Each one carries a domain, a skill list, a difficulty, a full explanation, and — where it helps — an analysis of why each wrong answer is tempting.

On scoring: College Board does not publish the tables used to convert a raw score into the 200–800 scale, and the real conversion depends on which second module you received. The scale used here is a documented model that reproduces the behaviour that matters for practice — it is not official data, and scores should be read as a guide rather than a prediction.
